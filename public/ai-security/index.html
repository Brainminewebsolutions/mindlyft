Questions Learners Frequently Ask

Everything you need to know about batch timings, prerequisites, offline lab facilities, and C3iHub IIT Kanpur co-certification.

What is the course fee, and do you support corporate invoicing?
Applications of AI, AI Security & Governance is offered as a specialized module cluster included directly within the 60 Hour Advanced Level (₹75,000 + GST) and the 100 Hour Combo Certification (₹1,20,300 + GST). We offer GST invoicing for corporate employee upskilling budgets, group cohort discounts for enterprise teams of 3 or more, and 3 to 12-month no-cost EMI options starting from approximately ₹5,000/month.
Is deep Python programming required for this track?
Basic familiarity with Python is helpful for running evaluation scripts in Microsoft PyRIT and AIF360, but deep machine learning engineering is not required. The course provides turnkey Jupyter notebooks, pre-configured execution environments, and focused governance frameworks tailored for risk officers, security architects, and technical leads alike.
How does this compare to standard ISO 27001 lead auditor courses?
ISO 27001 focuses broadly on information security management systems (ISMS) like firewalls, backups, and physical access. It has zero provisions for probabilistic AI risks, prompt injections, or training data bias. ISO 42001 is the world's dedicated standard for Artificial Intelligence Management Systems (AIMS), and this course pairs theoretical compliance with hands-on red teaming.

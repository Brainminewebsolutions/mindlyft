What makes this course different from other cybersecurity institutes in Pune?
Three distinct differentiators: (1) Official co-branded certification from C3iHub, IIT Kanpur (India's national cybersecurity hub under NM-ICPS); (2) Senior C3iHub faculty visits Pune and teaches in-person in our classroom; (3) Live bare-metal hardware labs (Palo Alto PA-VM firewalls, Splunk SIEM clusters, and AI red teaming with Claude MCP and PyRIT) instead of multiple-choice exam dumps.
What are the fees for Level 1, Level 2, and the 100-Hour Combo?
The AI in Cybersecurity Combo track (100 Hours) fee is ₹1,20,300 + GST (saving ₹4,700 compared to taking Level 1 at ₹50,000 and Level 2 at ₹75,000 separately). We offer zero registration fee, early-bird merit scholarships, and flexible 3 to 12-month no-cost EMI options starting from approximately ₹10,025/month. Can I enroll if I don't have prior cybersecurity experience?
Yes. The Full-Stack Cybersecurity track begins with foundational Level 1 offensive security and networking before advancing into Palo Alto firewalls, Splunk SIEM, and AI governance. Basic familiarity with computer networks or programming is helpful, but our pre-bootcamp prep modules will get you up to speed.
How does placement assistance work?
MindLyft AI operates an active corporate hiring network across Pune, Bengaluru, and Hyderabad, including GCCs, banks, MSSPs, and C3iHub-incubated cybersecurity startups. Students receive resume restructuring, mock technical interviews, and direct interview referrals.

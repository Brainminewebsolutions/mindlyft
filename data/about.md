<H1> About MindLyft AI <h1>
MindLyft AI is an AI education and solutions company based in Pune. We teach people to build
with artificial intelligence, and we help organisations put it to work.
Most of what we do happens in a classroom at our Akurdi campus: hands-on programmes in
generative AI, machine learning, data analytics and cybersecurity, taught in person by people
who work in these fields. Alongside that, our solutions team builds AI systems for businesses —
automation, analytics and custom software.
The two sides inform each other. What we learn delivering real projects goes straight back into
what we teach.
<H2> Why we started <H2>
(New section. Place directly after the intro.)
Our founder's starting point was simple: AI is not just a technology, it is a force that can raise
what people are capable of — but only if they can actually use it.
Too much AI education stops at vocabulary. People finish courses able to describe a large
language model and unable to build anything with one. MindLyft AI was set up to close that gap,
with teaching built around projects, instructors who practise what they teach, and a campus
where you can ask a question the moment you are stuck.

H3 — AI education and training
Classroom programmes in agentic AI and LLMs, machine learning, data analytics and applied
AI tools, from beginner level to advanced. Every programme is built around real projects, so
learners leave with a portfolio rather than only a certificate.
H3 — IIT Kanpur programmes, with C3iHub
A dedicated track delivered in collaboration with C3iHub at IIT Kanpur, running from beginner
through advanced levels, with instructors drawn from C3iHub's own team. The cybersecurity
programmes cover ethical hacking, threat intelligence, digital forensics and incident response.
Programmes on this track carry a joint MindLyft AI and C3iHub IIT Kanpur certification.
H3 — AI solutions for business
For organisations, we build machine learning models, intelligent automation, data analytics
systems and custom software — designed around a measurable business outcome rather than
technology for its own sake.

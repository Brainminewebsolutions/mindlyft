Intro paragraphs (no heading)
Most people learning generative AI today can describe what an LLM does. Far fewer can build a
retrieval pipeline that survives messy real data, explain why they chose one chunking strategy
over another, or ship an agent that completes a multi-step task without falling over.
That second group is who companies are hiring. This programme was built to get you there.

You are not dropped into transformers on day one — Module 1 starts with Python, and by
Module 4 you are building RAG-based enterprise applications and agentic systems with
LangChain and LangGraph. Five modules, 38 lessons, ending in capstone projects you can put
in front of an employer.

What is the difference between generative AI and agentic AI?
Generative AI covers models that produce content — text, code, images — in response to a
prompt. Agentic AI builds on that: systems that plan, use tools, hold memory and complete
multi-step tasks with limited supervision. Both are covered here, with agentic AI and LangGraph
taught in Module 4.

full machine learning project and hands-on NLP work.

What a session actually looks like
Two to three hours, capped at a small group so you can actually ask questions.
We start with the problem, not the tool — what this technology is genuinely good at, and where it falls over. Then you open a laptop and build something small that works. You leave with the file you made, the steps to repeat it, and an honest answer to whether this is worth your time.
There is no sales presentation at the end. If you want to talk about a full programme, our counsellors are in the building and you can ask. If you do not, nobody will chase you.

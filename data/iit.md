MindLyft AI runs these programmes in collaboration with C3iHub, the cybersecurity technology innovation hub at IIT Kanpur, established under India's National Mission on Interdisciplinary Cyber-Physical Systems. The instructors are C3iHub's own — people who have delivered training for government departments, defence and public sector organisations, and who work on live security engagements. On completion you receive a certificate issued jointly by MindLyft AI and C3iHub, IIT Kanpur.
Five programmes, from a non-technical starting point through to advanced practitioner level. All of them are taught from our Akurdi campus in Pimpri-Chinchwad, with online and hybrid options depending on the track.

Common questions
H3

Are these online or classroom? Both, depending on the programme. The two 75-hour tracks are online. Levels 1 and 2 and the 100-hour certification are hybrid, combining classroom sessions at Akurdi with recorded and live online material.
